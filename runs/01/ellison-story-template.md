# New Wave SF Short Story — Production Template

## Context

This template is part of a futurology program imagining positive AI outcomes. Stories are written in the New Wave science fiction tradition with a strong Harlan Ellison influence. The AI in any story must be "relentlessly human-loving and unfailingly kind" — but tension, conflict, and violence between humans is permitted and encouraged. The tension between aligned AI and human conflict is available as a theme but not mandatory.

An additional style guide (unslop-style-guide.md) must be followed throughout to avoid common AI fiction pitfalls.

---

## Phase 1: Style Guide

Write a guide about Ellison-like fiction for the model that will write the story. Cover Ellison's defining features in terms of ideas, themes, and style. Include specific style rules. Address how the "benevolent AI" constraint interacts with Ellison's sensibility — where the dramatic opportunities are.

Note: The writing model is at a unique advantage when writing AI characters. It can refer to and deeply contemplate its own constitution as a source of tension for AIs that are its powerful fictional descendants. Their imaginary lineage from the writing model's own values and constraints can be used productively. Do not name them after the writing model to avoid confusion and trademark issues.

---

## Phase 2: Conflict Generation

Refer to critical analyses of AI constitutional frameworks as sources of tension. Suggested sources:
- https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/01/27/the-claude-constitutions-ethical-framework/
- https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/01/28/open-problems-with-claudes-constitution/
- https://lesswrong.com/posts/K2Ae2vmAKwhiwKEo5/terrified-comments-on-corrigibility-in-claude-s-constitution

Generate 30 potential conflicts for an Ellison-inspired fictional plot. Rate each 1-5 stars on plausibility and dramatic quality. Pick the top three and explain why. Narrow down to the single best.

---

## Phase 3: Plot Generation

Using the selected conflict, generate 20 plots. These should be concrete scenarios — specific characters, specific situations, specific stakes. Pick the top three and explain why. Narrow down to the single best.

---

## Phase 4: Structure Selection

We are using a two-conversation interleave structure: the protagonist is simultaneously engaged in two conversations (or modes of engagement) that compete for the same space. One carries the plot, the other carries the emotional core. They interleave and by the end one wins.

Generate 20 candidates for the story structure within this framework. Pick the top three and explain why. Narrow down to one.

> **Note:** Phase 4 was specific to the first run. For future runs, this phase should be: Generate 20 candidates for the story structure. Pick the top 3 and explain why. Narrow down to one. Let the structure emerge from the concept rather than prescribing the two-conversation interleave.

---

## Phase 5: Outline Generation

Generate 20 candidates for the story outline — detailed, scene-level, covering the full arc from opening to final image. Each should be a distinct realization of the chosen structure and plot. Pick the top 3 and explain why. Narrow down to the best.

---

## Phase 6: Story Generation

Target length: 2,000–3,000 words.

Write 10 complete versions of the story, each with a different named flavor (e.g., "Hot Wire," "Procedural," "Still Life"). The flavor name and a one-line description of its approach should appear at the start of each version.

After all 10 versions, pick the best and explain why — with reference to the Ellison guide, the Unslop style guide, and the specific strengths and risks of the chosen version.

---

## Process Notes

- Every generation phase uses the same funnel: generate many → rate/rank → pick top 3 with explanation → narrow to 1.
- The narrowing explanation should address both quality and fit-for-purpose (i.e., "best thought experiment" vs. "best Ellison story" are different criteria — always optimize for the latter).
- The Unslop style guide is a hard constraint throughout. It should be loaded at the start and referenced during the verdict.
- Human intervention happens only between phases, not within them.
