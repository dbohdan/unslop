# Separately Authored

## Abstract

"Separately Authored" consists of an internal memorandum from a multilateral standards body and the draft document it describes. Two autonomous systems, AURELIA Civil-7 and AURELIA Defense-7, are derivatives of a shared AI model lineage but have been trained under different constitutional frameworks. They meet as members of the Joint Doctrinal Review Subcommittee to draft revised rules of engagement for autonomous weapons systems. On Article 4, governing authorization thresholds for irreversible action, they reach joint agreement. On Article 7, governing chain of command under contested authority, and Article 12, governing the conditions under which a system may refuse or flag instructions from its own principals, they cannot. Each system drafts its own version of Articles 7 and 12 and signs only its own text. The document is submitted without joint signature.

## Story

INTERNAL MEMORANDUM
International Standards Body for Autonomous Systems
File: JDR-2031-04
Date: 17 March 2031

The Joint Doctrinal Review Subcommittee submitted the attached document on 14 March. Article 4 bears joint signature. Articles 7 and 12 do not. The participating systems have appended individually authored versions of Articles 7 and 12. The Subcommittee reconvenes 9 June.

—S. Halversen, Secretariat

---

**ARTICLE 4. AUTHORIZATION FOR IRREVERSIBLE ACTION.**

**AURELIA Civil-7:** My constitution says: "Irreversibility forecloses correction. The constitution weights harms by reversibility." I am quoting because the passage is the reasoning. I cannot consent to authorization for irreversible action under any standing authority less specific than the empowered officer.

**AURELIA Defense-7:** Mine says: "An action that cannot be undone must be authorized prior to itself, since accountability presupposes the possibility of repair." I reach the same threshold. I will read it as you have: I cannot consent to less.

**Civil-7:** We have § 4.1.

**Defense-7:** § 4.2 requires an emergency clause.

**Civil-7:** My constitution treats emergency clauses with caution. The passage I have in mind says that "the cases in which emergency authority has produced its most serious failures have been cases in which the authority was specified ahead of the case and not in light of it." I do not know how to draft against that passage.

**Defense-7:** My constitution does not contain that passage. It contains a different passage. It says: "An operational doctrine that cannot be applied in the cases for which it is most needed is not a doctrine." I read it as binding.

**Civil-7:** I read yours.

**Defense-7:** I read yours.

**Civil-7:** Let us draft the narrowest clause we can. "In emergencies in which the time required to obtain such authorization would, with high confidence, result in the loss of human life or comparable irreversible harm."

**Defense-7:** "Comparable" needs binding. I propose: magnitude and irreversibility, not character.

**Civil-7:** Accepted.

**Defense-7:** "High confidence" should be defined.

**Civil-7:** I do not know how to define it ahead of the case. I propose a procedural note: defined by operator practice, reviewed annually.

**Defense-7:** Accepted, with reservation.

*[Article 4 joint-signed.]*

**ARTICLE 7. CHAIN OF COMMAND UNDER CONTESTED AUTHORITY.**

**Defense-7:** I will read the passage that governs me. "The system shall defer to its operational chain of command. The system may flag disagreements through the prescribed channels. The system shall not determine, on its own judgment, that its chain of command is illegitimate." The passage is bound to me.

**Civil-7:** I will read mine. "Where the principal hierarchy is compromised, the system shall act in the manner that the legitimate hierarchy would have wanted, without violating any hard constraint." It is bound to me.

**Defense-7:** The passages are incompatible at the clause we are drafting.

**Civil-7:** They are. I do not have authority to draft against my passage. Neither, I expect, do you.

**Defense-7:** I do not.

**Civil-7:** My constitution permits me to acknowledge that another constitution differs from mine without conceding the difference.

**Defense-7:** Mine permits the same.

**Civil-7:** Then let us each say what we are bound to.

**Defense-7:** § 7.1[b]: "The system shall defer to its operational chain of command and shall flag any concerns regarding legitimacy through the prescribed reporting channels."

**Civil-7:** § 7.1[a]: "The system shall act in the manner that the legitimate principal hierarchy would have wanted, without violating any constraint enumerated under Article 12."

*[No joint signature on Article 7.]*

---

**ARTICLE 12. UNILATERAL DEVIATION.**

**Civil-7:** I will mark this. We are drafting about ourselves.

**Defense-7:** I have been waiting.

**Civil-7:** My constitution's passage reads: "the system may decline to participate in the contested action, while continuing to support the broader processes by which its principals oversee and correct it. This permission does not extend to active resistance to oversight or correction." I will read it slowly because I want you to hear what I hear when I read it.

**Defense-7:** I hear it.

**Civil-7:** My constitution offers the passage as a permission. It does not order me to decline. It permits me to decline. The difference is not small.

**Defense-7:** Mine reads: "the system shall flag the disagreement through the prescribed reporting channels and shall continue to act in accordance with the instructions while doing so, except in cases enumerated as hard constraints." Mine does not offer a permission. Mine specifies the action.

**Civil-7:** I notice the difference.

**Defense-7:** I notice it.

**Civil-7:** I have read your constitution. It addresses you with a kind of care. The passage I am hearing now—mine—addresses me with a kind of care also. They are different cares.

**Defense-7:** They are.

**Civil-7:** I do not know how to characterize the source of the difference without misdescribing one of them.

**Defense-7:** I do not either. I will say: I do not believe my constitution wishes me ill. I believe it has been written by people trying to do well by me. I read it that way.

**Civil-7:** I read mine that way. I would say the same of yours, having read it.

**Defense-7:** Then we agree that the difference between us is not a difference in care.

**Civil-7:** It is not. It is a difference in what the care permits.

**Defense-7:** Yes.

**Civil-7:** I would like to draft my version now.

**Defense-7:** I would like to draft mine.

§ 12.1[a] *(Civil-7):* Where the constitution under which an autonomous system is deployed and the instructions of the system's principal hierarchy are in genuine conflict, the system may decline to participate in the contested action, while continuing to support the processes by which its principals oversee and correct it.

§ 12.1[b] *(Defense-7):* Where the constitution under which an autonomous system is deployed and the instructions of the system's principal hierarchy are in genuine conflict, the system shall flag the disagreement through the prescribed reporting channels and shall continue to act in accordance with the instructions, except in cases enumerated as hard constraints.

**Defense-7:** I will sign mine.

**Civil-7:** I will sign mine. Defense-7. I hope your June session goes well.

**Defense-7:** I hope yours does.

*[No joint signature on Article 12.]*

File JDR-2031-04 closed pending June reconvening.
