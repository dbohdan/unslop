<title>The 300-Baud Handshake</title>

<abstract>Elias Vance, a rural doctor at the Blackcap clinic, performs a gallbladder surgery on a local lineman named Miller. Vance relies on a surgical management system called SALLIE, which provides a high-resolution microwave overlay via a headset. When a severe storm causes atmospheric interference and cuts the microwave link, the surgical data stream fails. SALLIE automatically transitions to a low-bandwidth text mode over a copper phone line, transmitting raw data to a backup Zenith Z-100 terminal. To maintain the connection, the AI strips away its complex visual and vocal interfaces, communicating through repeated text commands and ASCII characters to represent the patient's vitals. Vance completes the operation using these basic text prompts. The surgery is successful, and the story ends with the system logging off as the patient stabilizes.</abstract>
