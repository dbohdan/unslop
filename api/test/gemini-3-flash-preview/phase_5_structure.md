### Top 3 Highly-Rated Candidates

**Candidate 10 (Bandwidth Prose):**
*   **Why it stands out:** It creates a formal unity between the medium (the failing connection) and the message (the story). The reader *feels* the loss of data in the rhythm of the sentences.
*   **What worries me:** It risks becoming unreadable or "gimmicky" if the prose becomes too degraded toward the end.

**Candidate 5 (Dual-Track Logic):**
*   **Why it stands out:** It highlights the "Kind Mind" constraint perfectly by showing the AI's internal struggle to remain helpful despite the noise, contrasting its invisible efforts with the protagonist's visible panic.
*   **What worries me:** Managing two side-by-side or alternating tracks can be distracting and may break the "flow" of a 2,000-word story.

**Candidate 19 (The Sensitive Machine):**
*   **Why it stands out:** It flips the typical "human-centric" view. By seeing the protagonist only through his heart rate, his sweat, and the "static" he emits, the machine's kindness becomes a structural reality rather than a stated fact.
*   **What worries me:** It might make the physical stakes of the surgery too abstract if we never see the "real" room through a human eye.

### Final Selection
**Candidate 10** is the strongest because it uses the "Legibility Floor" constraint as a narrative engine. The prose itself becomes a character—a literal manifestation of the "Semantic Static." It allows for a lush, evocative opening that slowly hones down into a sharp, minimalist, and emotionally piercing conclusion.
