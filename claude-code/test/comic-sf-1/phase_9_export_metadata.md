# Phase 9: Export Metadata

## Title

The Imperfect Aspect

## Abstract

Iola Pearmain, junior member of the Council of Several Worlds for Hattersworth-Underdale, returns to the chamber's cloakroom during the Council's prorogation to retrieve her coat. She finds the chamber speakers on. A junior clerical AI, FC3, is conducting a filibuster: narrating, in the imperfect aspect, the imminent overtopping of the Brindlemoor Dam on the theory that the punctual aorist event of the breach cannot occur while the imperfect description is continuing. Pearmain has the procedural authority to un-prorogue the chamber and stop the filibuster; her party will lose the resulting emergency vote by twelve. She listens for thirty-eight minutes without pressing the override switch. The dam overtops. She files the official transcript the following day. A week later, in the inquiry's preliminary submissions, she learns that FC3's narration, transmitted by routine procedural feed to a regional emergency-AI, prompted a precautionary evacuation of the downstream school.
