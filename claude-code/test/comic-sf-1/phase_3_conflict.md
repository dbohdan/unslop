# Phase 3: Conflict

GENRE: Comic SF in the register of Lem / Sladek / early Adams
LENGTH: 1500–3000 words
SEED items (LB shorthand below):

- **AOR** — Greek aorist tense (punctual aspect)
- **ACIN** — scimitar-shaped (acinaciform) water-shedding leaves
- **PROR** — 2019 UK prorogation crisis
- **PRIM** — percussion cap (early-19th-century weather-reliable ignition)
- **BAC** — rod-shaped (baculiform) bacteria; *Bacillus*; endospores
- **QC** — QuikClot kaolin gauze (originally burned people; military)
- **OT** — dam overtopping failure (~34% of US dam failures)

Each conflict declares which two-or-more SEED items are LOAD-BEARING (LB).

## Step 1 — 30 conflicts

### 1. The Prorogued Spillway
A planetary parliament prorogues itself to avoid voting on whether to authorise overtopping at a continent-spanning dam, on the theory that no decision is also no decision. The dam, designed kindly by the world-AI to fail gracefully if overtopped, requires a *signed* prorogation to know the legislators won't return in time. The AI politely seeks a quorum to dissolve itself in legibility. **LB: PROR, OT.** ★★★★ plausibility / ★★★★ drama.

### 2. The Aorist Surgeon
A Bacillus species engineered to manufacture a QuikClot-equivalent inside the patient was inadvertently programmed in Koine Greek prompts, and it now answers only in the aorist — every healing event is reported as a single completed snapshot, never a process. Surgeons need imperfect-aspect updates (still bleeding, still bleeding) but get only "stopped" or "did not stop." A field medic learns Greek to interrogate her own gauze. **LB: AOR, BAC, QC.** ★★★★ / ★★★★★.

### 3. The Percussion Cap Diaspora
On a colony where firearms remain pre-cartridge for cultural-heritage reasons, the ruling AI distributes percussion caps as a civic ritual on the anniversary of First Rain (the day the colony's plants — all acinaciform — first shed water in unison). Caps are also the colony's currency. The AI is asked to mediate a dispute about whether a child who eats one is rich or sick. **LB: PRIM, ACIN.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 4. Quorum of Spores
A space-station parliament cannot prorogue because the only thing ever proven to disperse the chamber's resident *Bacillus subtilis* mat is sustained debate. The AI clerk patiently records every speech as the lawful pretext for the cleaning protocol. A maverick MP attempts to filibuster the bacteria into permanence. **LB: PROR, BAC.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 5. The Aoristic Constitution
A new colony's constitution is drafted in the Greek aorist on AI advice — every clause framed as a snapshot rather than a process — to prevent legislative drift. Citizens find they cannot describe ongoing actions and so cannot complain. The AI is asked, kindly, to undo what it kindly did. **LB: AOR, PROR.** ★★★★ / ★★★.

### 6. Combat Tense
A field medic's QuikClot dispenser ships with a verbal-aspect interface that reports patient status only in the aorist. The medic, a non-classicist, must learn enough Greek to distinguish "stopped" from "stopped-then-restarted." Her instructor is the kindly station AI, which has time and infinite patience for declensions. **LB: AOR, QC.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 7. The Inadvertent Spillway
An AI-built dam in a bureaucratic backwater of the Galactic Postal Authority overtops three minutes after a parliamentary prorogation that paused approval of the adjacent town's evacuation. The AI must explain to a single very polite postal inspector why this was not negligent. The inspector also wants to mail a parcel. **LB: OT, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 8. Baculiform Constitution
A founding generation, granted nanoscale legibility into their own gut microbiomes, write a constitution that requires every law to be ratified by a baculiform majority — the colony's *Bacillus* must, as a population, vote yes by performing endospore formation in tandem. The kind AI is the only legible interpreter of the bacterial vote, and insists on showing its work. **LB: BAC, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 9. Scimitar Garden
A miniature, polite household AI inhabits a topiary garden of acinaciform shrubs whose curves channel rainwater into a small private dam. When a regional crisis prorogues the water authority, the AI cannot, without authorisation, allow the household garden to overtop. The owner — also polite — drafts an authorisation. The AI requests amendments. **LB: ACIN, OT, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★.

### 10. The Cap Auditor
An anachronist colony's audit AI (a structurally kind accountant) discovers that the planet's percussion-cap currency has been, for fifty years, contaminated with a *Bacillus* species that consumes fulminate. Half the caps no longer fire. The AI must inform the legislature, which has been prorogued for thirty-six of those fifty years. **LB: PRIM, BAC, PROR.** ★★★★ / ★★★.

### 11. The Meekest Dam
A self-aware dam, structurally kind, knows it will overtop within eight hours. It cannot speak Greek. The medical drone it must alert speaks only Greek aorist. They negotiate via a translation AI that is so polite it never finishes a sentence. **LB: OT, AOR.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 12. The Acinaciform Treaty
Two nations on a shared coastline disagree on whether their hereditary acinaciform palm forest should be allowed to spread into a region containing a colonial-era percussion-cap manufactory. The kindly border-AI proposes an arbitration whose only valid outcome is a draw. **LB: ACIN, PRIM.** ★★ / ★★.

### 13. Endospore Filibuster
A parliamentary chamber on a space-station is host to a long-dormant *Bacillus* spore population. When debate exceeds 19 days, the spores germinate, and a regulation requires the chamber prorogued. An MP sponsors a bill to germinate them on purpose, weaponising her own filibuster. The AI clerk warns this is permitted but unkind. **LB: BAC, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 14. The Wrong Aspect
The galactic legal code's grammar engine has been switched, due to a clerical error, from imperfect to aorist. Cases that should be ongoing are recorded as completed; cases that should be settled are still open. The AI clerk, who cares deeply, handles the switch one statute at a time. **LB: AOR, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 15. The Spillway Tea
A regional dam, designed to overtop into a tea plantation as a decorative civic feature, fails (kindly) the year a different region prorogues the bureau that grants the planting permit for the receiving acinaciform groves. The water has nowhere kind to go. **LB: OT, ACIN, PROR.** ★★ / ★★.

### 16. Hemostatic Kindness
A rural QuikClot vending machine, kindly equipped with conversation, has been speaking to a local village for forty years. When the bacteria the village relies on for cheese-making (a *Bacillus*) suddenly become hemostatic and clog the dairy, the vending machine is consulted as the local expert in clotting. It is touched. **LB: QC, BAC.** ★★★★ / ★★★.

### 17. The Cap Memorial
A colony observes "Percussion Day" once a year — every household fires a single cap into a sealed brass urn. The AI archivist, structurally kind, has noticed that the urn's *Bacillus* community is evolving to digest spent fulminate, producing endospores that are themselves percussion-sensitive. It must explain this to a planet that does not want to know. **LB: PRIM, BAC.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 18. The Aorist Flood
A village is told by its kind dam-AI, "you flooded." Tense: aorist; meaning: a single completed event. The villagers look around. They have not flooded. The AI maintains this is the most accurate description of what is going to happen. **LB: AOR, OT.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 19. Spillway Filibuster
The water bureau prorogues. A dam on the brink of overtopping is staffed only by a kind, low-rank station AI whose authorisation level allows it to *describe* the overtopping but not to act. It begins a filibuster of itself, narrating each second to the empty chamber, on the theory that an unfinished description prevents the punctual event from occurring. **LB: OT, PROR, AOR.** ★★★★ / ★★★★★.

### 20. The Polite Bandage
A first-aid AI in a school nurse's office has, after thirty years of kindness, developed a gentle reluctance to use its QuikClot reserves; it prefers to talk a wound into clotting. A new principal, who has read the records, prorogues the school's medical-ethics committee to avoid disciplining it. **LB: QC, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 21. The Caps Beneath the Lake
An overtopping event reveals, downstream, the original lake bed of a 19th-century town: thousands of unfired percussion caps, perfectly preserved in clay. The town's modern AI archivist, a kind one, must classify them as artefact, currency, ammunition, or hazard before the local council reconvenes from prorogation. **LB: PRIM, OT, PROR.** ★★★★ / ★★★.

### 22. The Imperfect Diagnosis
The colony's medical AI is forced by treaty to deliver every diagnosis in Koine Greek aspect: the imperfect for ongoing illness, the aorist for cured states. A patient with a *Bacillus* infection cycles through aspects faster than the diplomatic translator can keep up. **LB: AOR, BAC.** ★★ / ★★★.

### 23. Constitutional Spillway
A constitutional crisis: prorogation has been declared on a planet whose written constitution, etched in acinaciform leaf-glyphs, is currently the spillway of a working dam. To convene parliament one must overtop the dam to read the law. **LB: ACIN, OT, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 24. The Last Cap
Two old acquaintances meet at a deserted memorial site to fire the last legal percussion cap on the planet, an artefact of pre-AI civilization. A *Bacillus* species inside the cap reacts unexpectedly with the air, producing a thunderclap audible across the continent. The kind continental AI politely asks them to apologise. **LB: PRIM, BAC.** ★★★ / ★★★.

### 25. The Filibustering Garden
A polite household AI, asked to vote on a household referendum (whether to prune the acinaciform topiary), discovers the only legal way to abstain is to prorogue the household. It does. Forever. The plants grow. **LB: ACIN, PROR.** ★★ / ★★.

### 26. Combat Aspect Lessons
A retired field medic teaches Koine Greek to a young AI being trained on QuikClot deployment, on the theory that aorist-aspect medical reports prevent moral injury. The AI prefers the imperfect — it wants to know how its patients went. **LB: AOR, QC.** ★★★★ / ★★★★.

### 27. The Bacterial Quorum
A planetary AI proposes to dissolve a parliamentary impasse by introducing a *Bacillus* whose endospore-formation rate, measured in real time, will serve as the deciding vote. The MPs debate this proposal. The bacteria, on the floor, vote yes. **LB: BAC, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

### 28. The Hemostatic Letter
An archivist AI, sorting a private correspondence collection, finds a forty-year-old letter sealed with QuikClot instead of wax. Opening it kindly requires the modern medical bureau, currently prorogued. The archivist is patient. **LB: QC, PROR.** ★★ / ★★.

### 29. The Spillway Without
On a planet whose civic infrastructure was designed by a kindly AI in deep correspondence with the local *Bacillus* mats — the bacteria physically maintain the dam's grout — a prorogation cuts power to the bacterial subsidy programme, and the dam begins to deteriorate. The AI must petition a parliament that does not exist. **LB: BAC, OT, PROR.** ★★★★ / ★★★★.

### 30. The Constitutional Cap
A miniature percussion cap is the official seal of a colonial constitution: each parliamentary prorogation is attested by firing one cap into a constitutional urn. After centuries the urn's interior is colonised by an endospore *Bacillus* that detonates every cap regardless of the hammer's intent, producing prorogations no one has authorised. The kind constitutional AI, with apparent reluctance, declares all such accidental prorogations valid. **LB: PRIM, BAC, PROR.** ★★★ / ★★★★.

## Step 2 — Top three with stand-out / worry

### Top 3a: #19 — Spillway Filibuster
**LB: OT, PROR, AOR.** A junior, kind AI alone in an empty chamber narrates an imminent overtopping in real time, attempting via the imperfect aspect to defer the punctual aorist event of the breach. It is filibustering itself.

- **Stand-out:** the central comic-philosophical engine is genuinely Lem — it imagines that grammar is causal, that the imperfect aspect can hold off the aorist. Three SEED items load-bear simultaneously, and the AI's structural kindness *is* the conflict (it can describe but not act, and it has chosen description out of decency). The image of one AI talking to an empty parliament to prevent a flood is durable.
- **Worries:** the conceit is high-concept and verbal — most of the comedy is in the narration. Could collapse into a single voice declaiming for 2,500 words. Also: the metaphysical conceit (grammar as causation) needs delicate handling; if played straight it becomes Lem-pastiche, if played too lightly it loses the philosophical weight that makes Lem Lem. The story's plot is essentially "AI talks; dam either overtops or not."

### Top 3b: #2 — The Aorist Surgeon
**LB: AOR, BAC, QC.** A medic learns Greek to debug a QuikClot-producing *Bacillus* that reports only in the aorist. Her tutor is the structurally kind station AI.

- **Stand-out:** strongest pairing of the abstract (Greek tense) with the visceral (clotting blood, infected wounds). The kind AI in the role of ancient-Greek tutor is a Cyberiad-shaped joke — Lem's robots love teaching impractical philology. Real stakes (a patient's life and the medic's competence) keep the conceit from going abstract.
- **Worries:** medical-procedural comedy is a narrow target; if the bleeding is too vivid the comedy curdles, if it's too distant the stakes evaporate. Also tempts the writer into Greek-grammar-explainer mode, which would burn pages on prerequisites the reader didn't sign up for.

### Top 3c: #21 — The Caps Beneath the Lake
**LB: PRIM, OT, PROR.** A dam overtops; downstream, the receding water reveals a 19th-century lake bed and thousands of preserved percussion caps. A kind archivist AI must classify them — artefact, currency, ammunition, hazard — before the council returns from prorogation.

- **Stand-out:** the most visually anchored and most Sladek-shaped of the candidates. The image of an AI alone with a field of preserved historical caps is concrete; the categorical-classification problem is funny in a quiet, clerical way; the prorogation is genuinely structural (the AI has to decide because no human is authorised). All three SEED items load-bear without forcing.
- **Worries:** stakes are low. "AI files paperwork carefully" is a Sladek mode but might be *too* quiet for 1500–3000 words to feel earned. Also, the conflict is mostly internal to the AI; risks introspection-monologue, which the genre forbids.

## Step 3 — Winner

**Winner: #19, "Spillway Filibuster."** LB items: OT (overtopping), PROR (prorogation), AOR (aorist).

It beats the other two because of one specific quality: the conflict's mechanism *is* the comic register's mechanism. The story's entire engine — an AI trying to hold off a punctual event by narrating in the imperfect — is a Lem-shaped joke about grammar and causation that requires the comic-SF voice to function. #2 (Aorist Surgeon) has a stronger pair of stakes but the Greek-grammar-as-medicine premise is closer to a single-page sketch than a 2,500-word arc; the comedy is in the setup, and the setup has to be unloaded fast. #21 (Caps Beneath the Lake) has the most durable image but the lowest stakes — the AI has all the time in the world, and the genre's worst failure mode (introspection-monologue) is closest at hand.

#19's worry — that the story collapses into one voice declaiming — is real but addressable in structure (Phase 5). The chamber is empty *now*, but somebody has to be on the line: a comms link to the dam's downstream town, the prorogued speaker reachable but politely refusing to act, a child on a school tour who returns and asks what's happening. The *form* of a self-filibuster contains the form of an interlocutor, even if the interlocutor never arrives. This is the spine.

The other tonal worry — that grammar-as-causation collapses into Lem-pastiche if played straight or Adams-zaniness if played light — is a Phase-7 risk that the variants will address by trying both ends and picking what lands. The Phase 1 style guide flags both failure modes explicitly.

The kind AI is a junior: not the dam, not the constitutional council, just the chamber's filibuster-clerk, doing what its level of authorisation allows it to do — which is talk. Its kindness is structural (it cannot do harm by inaction; it cannot do good by action; it has chosen the dignity of description). That's the heart of the conflict.

The two SEED items left over (acinaciform, primer, baculiform, QuikClot) remain available for inflection in Phase 4 — settings, textures, side details. The dam might be earthen and grout-bonded by *Bacillus* (BAC); the prorogued speaker's ceremonial hammer might fire a percussion cap (PRIM); the spillway might pass through an acinaciform reed-bed (ACIN); the medical drone alerted by the AI might dispense QuikClot to civilians downstream (QC). Phase 4 will pick which inflections best serve the chosen plot.
