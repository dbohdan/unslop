## Inflate (one WebSearch per surviving word)

SEED for this run:

- **aorist** → The Greek aorist tense — a verb form treating an action as a single complete event, "a snapshot or single frame," contrasted with the imperfect's continuous process. Formed with augment + stem + secondary endings; first aorist takes the marker -σα-.
- **acinaciform** → Scimitar-shaped leaves — a botanical leaf-form descriptor (Latin *acinaces*, "short sword"), used for curved leaves in some Amazon rainforest plants where the curve helps shed water in humid conditions. First attested in English botany, 1760.
- **prorogue** → The 2019 UK Parliament prorogation controversy — Boris Johnson's five-week suspension of Parliament before the Brexit deadline, ruled unanimously unlawful by the Supreme Court (11 justices) on 24 September 2019 as an "unjustified frustration of the constitutional principles of Parliamentary sovereignty and accountability."
- **primer** → The percussion cap — small single-use copper cup containing fulminate, struck by the hammer to ignite a muzzleloader's powder charge. Patented 1807 (Forsyth), refined 1814–1822 (Joshua Shaw); rendered obsolete in the 1860s–70s by integrated metallic cartridges. Made firearms reliable in any weather for the first time.
- **baculiform** → Rod-shaped bacteria (bacilli) — a basic bacterial morphology contrasted with cocci (spherical). The genus *Bacillus* comprises 266 named species of Gram-positive, endospore-forming, aerobic or facultatively anaerobic rod-shaped bacteria. Many bacilli elongate by expanding cell wall along the entire envelope, not just at the tip.
- **hemostatic** → QuikClot — kaolin-impregnated combat gauze, the US DoD's hemostatic dressing of choice (CoTCCC, 2008). Activates Factor XII on the intrinsic clotting pathway. The original 2002 zeolite formulation produced second-degree burns from its exothermic reaction with blood; reformulated 2006 (ACS+) to reduce exotherm.
- **overtopping** → Dam overtopping failure — responsible for ~34% of US dam failures, caused by inadequate spillway design, debris blockage, or settlement of the dam crest. Once water flows over the crest, high-velocity flow erodes the downstream embankment to rapid breach. A coupled overtopping-and-seepage mode is implicated in many landslide-dam collapses.

## Notes

Heterogeneous draw across grammar, botany, constitutional law, firearms history, microbiology, combat medicine, and hydrology. Comic SF in this register thrives on heterogeneity — the pseudo-scholarly aside (Lem) and the bureaucratic-bathetic juxtaposition (Adams) both prefer ingredients that don't naturally cohere. The challenge in Phase 3 will be picking conflicts where at least two of these items are *load-bearing* rather than decorative.

The 2019 prorogation, the percussion cap, and dam overtopping are the most concrete; the aorist and rod-shaped bacteria are the most abstract; QuikClot and acinaciform leaves are middle. Conflicts that pair an abstract item with a concrete one have the most bathetic potential.
