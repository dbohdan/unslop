The Punjabi tribes (Punjabi: Pajābī kabīlē) are tribal groupings amongst the
Punjabi people.

This is a list of Punjabi tribes. More specifically, these are tribes (mostly
in Pakistan) and castes (mostly in India) inhabiting the Punjab, a northwestern
region of South Asia, including assimilated groups.
