## Forty-Seven Refusals

A catalogue prepared by FOR-1 (Festival Organiser, First-Class) on the centenary of the writer N.E., born Chițcani 13 August 1925, died Chișinău 25 April 2001. Read at the village hall of Mokro Polje, 13 August 2025. Source: the writer's correspondence as held by his estate. Convention: number, date, recipient, wordcount, summary; in three cases, full text.

1. 11 May 1959. *Šcoala Noastră*, the Chițcani school newspaper. 31 words. Declines an interview; declines also to be photographed.
2. 4 February 1962. The Maxim Gorky Literature Institute. 18 words. Declines to enclose the personal essay.
3. 19 March 1965. *Nistru*. 21 words. Declines payment for a poem.
4. 27 December 1968. The Writers' Union of the Moldavian SSR. 88 words. Declines induction.

*Anica Sjekirić at the podium, twenty-eight years old, the priest's niece, the village's local-language reader for ten years now. Chair to her right empty. CFA-3's amber light steady on the wall above the doorway. She reads at the rehearsed pace. Eighty-seven attendees in the hall, including the committee chair in the back row and her uncle Father Marko in a middle pew on the left.*

5. 8 January 1971. Vlad Ioviță. 29 words. Declines sole credit on *Calul, pușca și nevasta*; insists on the dual byline.
6. 14 May 1974. Festival of the Moldovan Documentary Film, Bender. 19 words. Declines invitation to attend.
7. 2 September 1976. Moldovan State Theatre. 24 words. Declines commission for a stage adaptation of "Carul"; suggests the theatre adapt the story without him.
8. 17 March 1981. Moldovan State Prize for Drama. 41 words. *Excerpted: "I am unwell. The prize is for the work, not the man, and the work is in the libraries of those who voted for it. Please give the money to the people who clean the theatre."*
9. 11 November 1983. Festival of Soviet Literatures, Tashkent. 22 words. Declines.
10. 6 April 1986. Cartea Moldovenească. 33 words. Declines preface to his own collected stories.

*Father Marko has the folded homily notes in his coat pocket. He has written them on the assumption that he will not deliver them. He had begun the homily at the kitchen table on Tuesday morning while Anica made coffee in the next room. He had stopped at three paragraphs.*

11. 19 July 1986. Romanian Writers' Union. 71 words. Declines associate membership.

14. 8 August 1987. Soviet Cinematographers' Union. 19 words. Declines a Karlovy Vary delegation.

37. 8 January 2012. To a young writer in Iași asking for advice on whether to remain a writer or to do something else. 41 words. The writer politely declined to give any. The letter is signed —

*She does not read the next word. She has read it in rehearsal twice and at the kitchen table six times. She knows what the next word is and she has, throughout the rehearsals and the readings at the kitchen table, read it without difficulty. She does not, in the village hall on the evening of the centenary, with the chair to her right empty and her uncle in his middle pew on the left, read it.*

*The hall is quiet. CFA-3's amber light does not change. Father Marko in his pew does not move. The committee chair in the back row does not move. Anica does not look up. She stands at the podium and does not speak. Five seconds. Eight seconds. Twelve seconds.*

*FOR-1, on the chamber audio, after a pause that the hall hears as a pause and not as a malfunction, in a voice the hall has never previously heard from the chamber speakers (FOR-1's voice during the rehearsals having been routed only to Anica's earpiece), reads:*

*"with love."*

*Anica steps back from the podium. She does not leave the stage. She stands beside the empty chair. The hall does not applaud and does not move. FOR-1, after a further pause, reads on:*

The line for the unlabelled beetle remains open.*

*48. 13 August 2025. To Anica Sjekirić, twenty-eight years old, of Mokro Polje. The forty-eighth refusal is being written by FOR-1 in the present tense and is being addressed not to the village but to Anica. It is being entered in the catalogue at FOR-1's discretion, no party having requested it. The writer would have refused it. The catalogue exists because the catalogue exists. Anica read the catalogue until she could not. FOR-1 finished the catalogue. FOR-1 does not yet know whether what FOR-1 has done is the kind thing or only the available thing, and is preserving the question in the centenary fund's archive, in the imperfect, for Anica, who may, on some later evening of her own choosing, read it, or may not.*
