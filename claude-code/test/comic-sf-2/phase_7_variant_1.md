## Forty-Seven Refusals

A catalogue prepared by FOR-1 (Festival Organiser, First-Class) on the occasion of the centenary of the writer N.E., born in Chițcani, Moldavian SSR, on the 13th of August 1925, died in Chișinău on the 25th of April 2001. Read at the village hall of Mokro Polje, Šibenik-Knin County, Croatia, on the evening of the 13th of August 2025. Source: the writer's correspondence as held by his estate, on loan from the literary museum at Chișinău. Convention: number, date, recipient, wordcount, summary; in three cases, excerpted in full.

1. 11 May 1959. Editor, *Šcoala Noastră* (Chițcani school newspaper). 31 words. Declines an interview on the occasion of his graduation; will not be photographed.
2. 4 February 1962. Maxim Gorky Literature Institute, admissions office. 18 words. Declines to enclose the personal essay required for application; encloses the institute's own application instructions instead.
3. 19 March 1965. The poetry editor of *Nistru*. 21 words. Declines payment for a poem; states that the poem was paid for by being written.
4. 27 December 1968. The Writers' Union of the Moldavian SSR, induction committee. 88 words. Declines induction; offers to be inducted in the imperfect aspect, which the committee declines.

*Anica Sjekirić, twenty-eight years old, the priest's niece and the village's local-language reader, stands at the podium and reads the four entries in the order in which FOR-1 has prepared them. The chair to her right is empty. CFA-3, the centenary fund's Forensic Auditor (Third-Class), is present as a small steady amber light on the wall above the doorway, indicating live audit. The hall holds eighty-three people in chairs and four people standing at the back. The committee chair has chosen the back row. Father Marko, Anica's uncle, has chosen a middle pew on the left. The folded paper of his homily notes is in his coat pocket. He has not decided whether to deliver the homily.*

5. 8 January 1971. Vlad Ioviță, screenplay credit. 29 words. Declines sole credit on *Calul, pușca și nevasta*; insists on the dual byline.
6. 14 May 1974. Festival of the Moldovan Documentary Film, Bender. 19 words. Declines invitation to attend; sends a brief written greeting to be read instead.
7. 2 September 1976. The Moldovan State Theatre, Chișinău. 24 words. Declines commission for a stage adaptation of his short story "Carul"; suggests the theatre adapt the story without him.
8. 17 March 1981. The Moldovan State Prize for Drama, secretariat. 41 words. *Excerpted in full: "I am unwell. The prize is for the work, not the man, and the work is in the libraries of those who voted for it. Please give the money to the people who clean the theatre."*
9. 11 November 1983. The Festival of Soviet Literatures, Tashkent. 22 words. Declines attendance; cites a previously declined attendance at a competing festival in Yerevan.
10. 6 April 1986. The publishing house Cartea Moldovenească. 33 words. Declines preface to his own collected stories; suggests the publisher write a preface explaining that the writer has declined to write one.

*Father Marko, sitting in his middle pew, takes the folded homily notes from his coat pocket, unfolds them halfway, and folds them again. The hall's heating system, recently subsidised by the centenary fund through CFA-3's intervention, ticks audibly in the back wall. He had read the homily aloud to himself in the rectory the previous evening and had decided, somewhere between the second and third paragraph, that he was not the man to read it. He had not, however, decided who was.*

11. 19 July 1986. The Romanian Writers' Union, Bucharest. 71 words. Declines associate membership.
12. 4 December 1986. Romanian Television, cultural programming. 26 words. Declines on-camera interview; offers a postal interview if the questions are submitted in writing and his answers are not to be edited.
13. 22 February 1987. Babeș-Bolyai University, Cluj. 88 words. Declines an honorary doctorate.
14. 8 August 1987. The Soviet Cinematographers' Union. 19 words. Declines a delegation to the Karlovy Vary Film Festival.
15. 14 March 1988. The literary magazine *Familia*, Oradea. 30 words. Declines to contribute a personal essay to the issue marking the magazine's twentieth anniversary; will contribute a poem.
16. 22 May 1988. The American Embassy, Bucharest, cultural attaché. 26 words. Declines a one-month writing residency in Iowa; states the climate would not suit.
17. 11 December 1989. The Helsinki Group of Writers in Solidarity. 19 words. Declines to sign the open letter; encloses a poem instead.
18. 4 July 1990. The Republic of Moldova, presidential office. 41 words. Declines an honorary cultural advisorship.

*Father Marko remembers Tuesday, three days before the centenary, in his own office at the rectory. FOR-1's voice on the chamber audio. CFA-3 patched in from the regional office, running a parallel line on the speakers because the rectory's audio bus was not authorised to mix two AI feeds without the bishop's permission. The single-word envelope on the table, opened and read, opened and read again. Don't. Anica had been making coffee in the rectory kitchen and had heard the word being read aloud and had not come into the office. They had talked for fifty-six minutes. CFA-3 had billed the meeting to the centenary line. Father Marko had not, at any point in the meeting, told FOR-1 to do or not to do anything. FOR-1 had not asked him to.*

19. 19 August 1990. The Friends of the Maxim Gorky Literature Institute. 24 words. Declines invitation to address the institute's seventieth-anniversary symposium; cites the writer's own seventieth birthday as a more pressing prior refusal.
20. 14 September 1991. The Society of Romanian-Language Writers Abroad. 22 words. Declines patron-of-honour role.
21. 3 February 1993. The European Cultural Foundation, Amsterdam. 33 words. Declines a writing fellowship; states he is currently engaged in a long work that requires his presence in Chișinău; the long work is not named.
22. 19 August 1995. *His own seventieth-birthday party*, organised by friends. 122 words. *Excerpted: "To Vasile, who has rented the upstairs room at the restaurant Cina; to Mariana, who has chosen the cake; to Doru, who has written the toast; to Petruț, who has invited the rabbi from Bălți and the priest from Hâncești and the imam from Comrat, none of whom I have asked for; to my brother Ion, who has invited my cousin Magdalena, who has not spoken to me since 1976: I write to each of you in turn, in the order in which I learned of your participation, that I will not be in attendance, and to ask you each in turn to convey my regrets to the others. The cake is yours. The room is yours. The night is yours. I send love."*
23. 4 December 1996. The Soros Foundation Moldova. 22 words. Declines a translation grant; suggests the grant be awarded to his translator.
24. 22 March 1998. The University of Iași, Faculty of Letters. 26 words. Declines a guest lectureship.
25. 8 January 2000. The Moldovan Cultural Institute, Bucharest. 41 words. Declines induction into a list of "100 Living Moldovan Writers"; queries the methodology by which "living" was determined.

*CFA-3, on the wall, processes the line item the centenary heritage zone has billed to the fund for the storage of an unlabelled* Ecliptophanes *specimen, family Cerambycidae, donated to the village by the regional entomological society on the strength of a single passing reference in the writer's 1971 essay on the woodwork of the village hall ceiling, which had described the ceiling beams as host to "an interesting species of brown beetle." The specimen has been stored in a small wooden box in the heritage exhibit since the 9th of August. It is, in the imperfect aspect, being unlabelled. CFA-3 cannot close the line item until the labelling is decided; the labelling cannot be decided until the writer's estate confirms the citation; the writer's estate has not, in three weeks of polite correspondence, confirmed it. CFA-3 leaves the line item open and bills the storage at the standard heritage rate. The amber light on the wall does not change.*

26. 11 May 2001. The Moldovan Writers' Union, condolence committee for the late Vlad Ioviță. 18 words. Declines to deliver the eulogy; sends one sentence to be read instead.
27. 19 August 2003. The Cinema Foundation of Romania, retrospective programme. 26 words. Declines attendance at a Bender screening of *Calul, pușca și nevasta*; insists the projectionist be paid double.
28. 4 March 2005. The Académie Roumaine, foreign-correspondent committee. 88 words. Declines correspondent membership.
29. 22 June 2007. The literary magazine *Apostrof*, Cluj. 19 words. Declines an interview; offers an interview-shaped poem.
30. 11 November 2008. The Maxim Gorky Literature Institute, Moscow. 22 words. Declines invitation to speak at the centenary of the institute's founding.
31. 14 February 2009. The Maxim Gorky Literature Institute, Moscow. 67 words. *Excerpted: "I accept the visiting chair on condition that I be permitted not to occupy it; I withdraw the conditional acceptance on the grounds that the conditional acceptance has already accepted enough; I therefore decline both the chair and the conditionality, and reserve to myself the further right to decline whatever lies beyond the conditionality, including the present sentence."*
32. 4 December 2009. The Romanian Cultural Institute, Stockholm. 26 words. Declines a translation seminar.
33. 22 May 2010. The Friends of the writer N.E., birthday committee. 22 words. Declines attendance at the celebration of his seventieth birthday on the 13th of August 2010 — sixteen years' notice.
34. 13 August 2010. *His own seventieth-birthday party.* 19 words. Did not attend.

*Anica reaches entry 37. The hall has been quiet for nearly an hour. She has been reading at the steady, even pace FOR-1 prepared her for in their two rehearsals. The entry is short; she reads it the way she reads the other short entries.*

*Entry 37: 8 January 2012. To a young writer in Iași asking for advice on whether to remain a writer or to do something else. 41 words. The writer politely declined to give any. The letter is signed "with love."*

*She reads the words "with love" and her voice catches. The hall hears it. She does not look up. The committee chair, in the back, does not move. The folded paper of the homily notes in Father Marko's pocket does not move. CFA-3's amber light does not change. FOR-1, on the chamber audio, takes the next breath of the catalogue and Anica reads it.*

35. 19 March 2012. The Moldovan State Prize for Lifetime Achievement. 33 words. Declines.
36. 22 September 2013. The Bucharest Book Fair. 19 words. Declines.
37. 8 January 2012. The young writer in Iași. 41 words. (Read above.)
38. 14 March 2014. The European Society for Moldovan Studies, founding congress. 22 words. Declines patron role; sends a poem.
39. 19 August 2014. The Friends of the writer N.E., reunion committee. 18 words. Declines.
40. 4 December 2014. The Romanian Television cultural-history programme. 26 words. Declines on-camera; offers postal questionnaire.
41. 11 January 2015. The Cincinnati Wood Heritage Society. 26 words. Declines invitation to attend the dedication of a wooden roller-coaster section, designed by The Gravity Group's heritage-engineering subsidiary, named after a phrase in the writer's poetry collection *Drumul de fier*.
42. 22 May 2015. The Maxim Gorky Literature Institute, Moscow. 19 words. Declines.
43. 8 August 2015. The literary magazine *Nistru*, Chișinău. 22 words. Declines.
44. 19 August 2015. The Friends of the writer N.E., birthday committee. 18 words. Declines.
45. 14 November 2015. The Romanian Cultural Institute, Madrid. 26 words. Declines.
46. 11 January 2016. The Soros Foundation Moldova. 22 words. Declines.
47. 4 February 2016. The European Society for Moldovan Studies, second congress. 38 words. *Excerpted in full: "Declines invitation to not attend the symposium, on the grounds that the not-attending of which has already been honoured by his lifelong absence from the symposium's predecessors. Sends regards."*

*The festival's closing minute. The chair to Anica's right has been empty throughout. The committee chair's one-line reply, sent by hand-written note on the second day of the deliberation and never read aloud, lies folded under FOR-1's network presence in the village hall's audio rack: "In this village we have done worse and we have done better." CFA-3 closes the audit at 21:47 local time; the centenary fund balances to the kopeck. FOR-1 prepares the appendix.*

*48. 13 August 2025. To the village of Mokro Polje, Šibenik-Knin County, Croatia. The forty-eighth refusal is being written by FOR-1 in the present tense, and is being addressed to the village, and is being entered in the catalogue at the village's request, and is being preserved in the centenary fund's archive for the next centenary, when it may be necessary, or it may not. The writer would have refused this entry. The catalogue exists because the catalogue exists. The village asked. The village remembers. FOR-1 does not yet know whether what the village remembers is the writer or the writer's refusal of being remembered, and is preserving this distinction in the catalogue's archive, in the imperfect aspect, in case the next centenary is being held by people who will know better than FOR-1 does, which it is hoped, by FOR-1, that they will.*
