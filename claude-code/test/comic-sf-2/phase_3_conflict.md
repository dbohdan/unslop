# Phase 3: Conflict Generation

GENRE: comic SF in the register of Lem / Sladek / early Adams.
TARGET LENGTH: 1000–3500 words.

SEED items (LB shorthand below):

- **FA** — forensic accounting (the discipline)
- **MP** — Mokro Polje (Croatian village; St. Luke's Orthodox Church 1524–1537; 1952 Partisan monument destroyed Nov 1996; pop. 163)
- **NE** — Nicolae Esinencu (1940–2016; Moldovan poet/screenwriter; "terrible child"; missed his own 70th; co-wrote *Calul, pușca și nevasta*). **Color-only.**
- **GG** — The Gravity Group (Cincinnati wooden coaster firm, 2002–; The Voyage, Hades 360, Boardwalk Bullet, Wooden Warrior, Cú Chulainn)
- **EC** — *Ecliptophanes* (genus of long-horn beetles, family Cerambycidae; 6 species)

Each conflict declares ≥2 LB items.

## Step 1 — 30 conflicts

### 1. The Coaster Audit
A galactic-postal subsidiary commissions The Gravity Group's design heirs to build a memorial coaster at a deep-time historic-restoration site (analogue of Mokro Polje). A structurally kind forensic-accounting AI is asked to audit the joint cost-share. The audit cannot reconcile a line item in the ride's payload coefficient. **LB: FA, GG, MP.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 2. The Monument Books
A reconstruction consortium reissues the destroyed 1952 Partisan monument at Mokro Polje as a touring sculpture. A forensic-accounting AI investigates the sculpture's restoration ledger and finds three different villages have charged it the same lodging fee. **LB: FA, MP.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 3. The Beetle Books
A taxonomic society's century-long *Ecliptophanes* monograph project is suddenly required to file a forensic-accounting report on its grant disbursements. The kind audit AI tries to reconcile the project's expenses, then asks why one species (E. tommyi) was funded but never described. **LB: FA, EC.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 4. The Esinencu Estate
The estate of a deceased writer in NE's tradition is being audited by a structurally kind forensic-accounting AI. The audit hinges on whether the writer's habit of skipping his own commemorations is a tax position or a personality. **LB: FA, NE.** ★★★ / ★★★★

### 5. The Wooden Coaster Beetle Crisis
A heritage wooden coaster (analogue of The Voyage) is being structurally compromised by a *Cerambycid* infestation in the load-bearing oak. The kind park-AI delays closure by reframing every inspection as an entomological survey. **LB: GG, EC.** ★★ / ★★★

### 6. The Re-enactment Audit
A small village stages an annual re-enactment of its 1996 monument-destruction. A forensic-accounting AI is the rotating treasurer of the re-enactment fund and politely asks whether the cost of the destroyed model can be amortised. **LB: FA, MP.** ★★★ / ★★★★

### 7. The Posthumous Coaster
A deceased screenwriter (NE-analogue) left in his will a clause requiring his ashes to be scattered along the lift hill of a specific wooden coaster. The Gravity Group's heir-firm and the writer's estate negotiate, with a kind notary AI mediating. **LB: NE, GG.** ★★ / ★★

### 8. The Beetle Auditor
The interplanetary forestry inspectorate uses a forensic-accounting AI trained originally on financial fraud to audit *Ecliptophanes* outbreak claims by colony orchards. The AI applies financial-accounting language to entomology, which the orchards find clarifying. **LB: FA, EC.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 9. The Cyrillic Ledger
A village (MP-analogue) keeps its civic accounts in Serbian Cyrillic by tradition; the regional forensic-accounting AI works only in Latin script and must translate every line item, asking the village priest as it goes. The breach: the priest's translation of a line for "monument" is "memory." **LB: FA, MP.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 10. The Coaster Beetle Audit
The Gravity Group's structural-engineering archive is found to contain twenty years of unbilled invoices to a beetle-removal subcontractor. A forensic-accounting AI begins the audit. Each invoice references a different *Ecliptophanes* species. **LB: FA, GG, EC.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 11. The Festival Fund
A small Carpathian village stages a writers' festival commemorating an Esinencu-figure, funded by the destroyed-and-rebuilt-monument's restoration grant. A kind forensic-accounting AI audits the festival's cash float and discovers the writer has, three times, posthumously declined to attend. **LB: FA, NE, MP.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 12. The Coaster Verses
A wooden roller coaster designed by the Gravity Group's heir-firm is decorated, by the local commune, with painted verses from a deceased Moldovan poet's lesser-known work. A kind translation AI is asked whether this constitutes a public reading. **LB: GG, NE.** ★★ / ★★★

### 13. The Beetle in the Books
A forensic-accounting AI auditing a small village (MP-analogue) library discovers an *Ecliptophanes* infestation in the 16th-century parish records. The audit must decide whether the beetles are evidence of damage or a parallel record-keeping system. **LB: FA, EC, MP.** ★★★ / ★★★★

### 14. The Cú Chulainn Inquiry
A wooden coaster named for an Irish hero (analogue of Cú Chulainn at Emerald Park) becomes the subject of a constitutional-heritage forensic-accounting inquiry: did the park, in commissioning the name, owe a fee to the cultural patrimony bureau? The kind AI inquirer files daily polite memoranda. **LB: FA, GG.** ★★ / ★★★

### 15. The Three Coasters of Mokro Polje
A reconstruction consortium proposes to rebuild the destroyed 1952 monument as one of three competing wooden coasters by The Gravity Group's heir-firm. A forensic-accounting AI is asked to score the proposals. The scores are identical. **LB: GG, MP, FA.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 16. The Estate's Coaster
The deceased Moldovan writer's estate, on its 30-year audit, is found to own 0.4% of a wooden coaster in Indiana. Neither the estate nor the park's kind AI can explain why. The forensic-accounting AI is asked to investigate. **LB: FA, NE, GG.** ★★★ / ★★★★

### 17. The Beetle Inheritance
A taxonomist's will leaves a small Croatian village a complete *Ecliptophanes* type collection. The village (MP-analogue) does not have a museum. A kind cataloguer AI is asked to recommend a use. **LB: EC, MP.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 18. Birthday with No Birthday
A village (MP-analogue) celebrates the centenary of a Moldovan writer (NE-analogue) who, in life, refused to attend his own celebrations. The village's kind festival AI must decide whether to honour his refusal posthumously. The forensic-accounting AI auditing the centenary fund is consulted on whether posthumous refusal is auditable. **LB: NE, MP, FA.** ★★★★★ / ★★★★

### 19. The Ledger of the Church
The 16th-century Orthodox Church of St. Luke at Mokro Polje is found to maintain, in its sacristy, a continuous parish-ledger from 1537 to the present, including entries for the 1952 monument and its 1996 destruction. A kind forensic-accounting AI is asked to audit the ledger as a closed historical artefact, then again as an open one. **LB: FA, MP.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 20. The Coaster Census
A planetary entomological census, structurally kind, is asked to count the *Ecliptophanes* populations in every wooden roller coaster on the planet. The Gravity Group's heir-firm cooperates, asking only that the census not include rides currently operating. **LB: EC, GG.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 21. The Forensic Festival
A small Croatian-style village hosts an annual festival of forensic accounting. Vendors include the regional auditor's office, a touring beetle-display, and the heirs of a wooden-coaster firm presenting a scale-model of a hypothetical memorial ride. A kind festival AI runs the schedule. **LB: FA, MP.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 22. The Posthumous Audit
A forensic-accounting AI is assigned to audit the literary estate of a deceased Moldovan-style writer; the audit's deadline is the writer's birthday, and the writer's heirs request the audit be filed as if the writer were attending it (which he would refuse to do). The AI files. **LB: FA, NE.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 23. The Carpathian Coaster
The Gravity Group's heir-firm is contracted to build a small wooden coaster at Mokro Polje as part of the restored 1952 monument's interactive heritage zone. The local forensic-accounting AI vets each invoice. The Esinencu Memorial Festival programmes the coaster's opening. **LB: GG, MP.** ★★★ / ★★★★ (heaviest SEED inflection, possibly too cluttered)

### 24. The Beetle and the Bishop
The Mokro Polje church's priest finds an *Ecliptophanes* specimen in the 17th-century icon screen. A kind beetle-taxonomy AI identifies the species; a kind forensic-accounting AI catalogues the discovery for ecclesiastical insurance. **LB: EC, MP, FA.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 25. The Ravine Flyer
A specific wooden coaster (Ravine Flyer II analogue), built over a Carpathian ravine, is the subject of a forensic-accounting dispute: the ravine is a heritage site; the coaster crosses it at a non-zero altitude. A kind AI mediates a question about whether altitude is heritage. **LB: GG, FA.** ★★ / ★★★

### 26. The Esinencu Beetle
A small genus of *Ecliptophanes* is named, by a Soviet entomologist of the 1970s, after a then-living Moldovan poet who, on hearing of the honour, refused to acknowledge it. The kind taxonomic AI is asked, fifty years later, whether to rename the genus. **LB: EC, NE.** ★★★ / ★★★★

### 27. The Translation Audit
A forensic-accounting AI auditing a Carpathian village's cultural-grant disbursement finds that one line item — for the translation of a Moldovan poem into Croatian — has been billed to the Gravity Group's heir-firm. Neither party admits the contract. **LB: FA, NE, GG.** ★★★ / ★★★

### 28. The Monument Ledger Beetle
The destroyed 1952 monument at Mokro Polje was discovered, when partially excavated in 2061, to contain inside its concrete plinth a complete continuous record (in beetle-bored channels in the wood inserts) of every villager's contribution to its construction. A kind forensic-accounting AI audits the *Ecliptophanes*-mediated ledger. **LB: EC, MP, FA.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 29. The Off-Stage Birthday
A festival (in MP) commemorating a Moldovan writer prepares a chair for him at the head table. The kind festival AI honours his lifelong refusal-of-celebration by reading the chair's empty agenda aloud. A forensic-accounting AI in the audience audits the chair as an asset. **LB: NE, FA, MP.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

### 30. The Inventory of Refusal
The estate of a deceased writer (NE-analogue) keeps an inventory of every honour and ceremony the writer refused in his lifetime. A kind forensic-accounting AI is asked to extend the inventory to include refusals he might have made if still alive. **LB: FA, NE.** ★★★★ / ★★★★

## Step 2 — Top three with stand-out / worry

### Top 3a: #18 — Birthday with No Birthday
**LB: NE, MP, FA.** A Croatian village celebrates the centenary of a Moldovan writer who, in life, refused to attend his own celebrations. The village's kind festival AI must decide whether to honour his refusal posthumously. The forensic-accounting AI auditing the centenary fund is consulted on whether posthumous refusal is auditable.

- **Stand-out:** all three SEED items load-bear in a way that produces the conflict's central comic-philosophical question: *what is the kind thing to do for someone who refused all kindness in his lifetime?* The genre's preferred shape (a small, specific situation that asks an unanswerable question) is fully present. The festival AI and the forensic-accounting AI as two-voiced kind agents create a Cyberiad-shaped duet. NE is honoured color-only — the writer is the absent centre, never on stage.
- **Worries:** centenary-festival comedy is a known niche and the writer's-refusal-of-his-own-celebration trope can read as twee. The risk is the story becoming a meditation on a writer's eccentricity rather than a comic SF piece. Length pressure too: at 1000–3500 words the festival, the village, the writer's biographical color, and two AIs all need real estate.

### Top 3b: #19 — The Ledger of the Church
**LB: FA, MP.** The 16th-century Orthodox Church of St. Luke at Mokro Polje keeps a continuous parish-ledger from 1537 to the present, including entries for the 1952 monument and its 1996 destruction. A kind forensic-accounting AI is asked to audit the ledger as a closed historical artefact, then again as an open one.

- **Stand-out:** the Mokro Polje historical density is fully exploited — five centuries of village ledger entries including the war-era destruction, audited by a kind AI in two registers (historical vs. live). Sladek-mode forensic comedy with Lem-mode philosophical depth (what's the difference between a closed and an open ledger?). The church and the AI as two custodians of memory is a strong pairing.
- **Worries:** only two SEED items load-bear. NE, GG, and EC all sit out. Possibly thinner than three-LB candidates. Also: parish-ledger comedy is structurally similar to comic-sf-1's stenographic-record-as-form work — risks repeating an earlier formal move.

### Top 3c: #29 — The Off-Stage Birthday
**LB: NE, FA, MP.** A festival (in MP) commemorating a Moldovan writer prepares a chair for him at the head table. The kind festival AI honours his lifelong refusal-of-celebration by reading the chair's empty agenda aloud. A forensic-accounting AI in the audience audits the chair as an asset.

- **Stand-out:** the empty-chair image is durable and visualisable. The two AIs serve different masters (festival-honour vs. financial-audit) and both arrive at versions of kindness that the writer himself would have refused. The tonal note — a roomful of AIs and humans politely commemorating someone who declined to be commemorated — is the project's central tone.
- **Worries:** structurally similar to #18 (same writer-refuses-own-honour pivot, same MP setting, same dual-AI cast). Single-scene risk: the entire conflict is a chair at a head table. May not sustain 1500–3500 words without thinning.

## Step 3 — Winner

**Winner: #18, "Birthday with No Birthday."** LB: NE, MP, FA.

It beats #19 because: #19 has only two LB items and uses the Mokro Polje material as setting rather than substance. The closed-vs-open-ledger conceit is good but distant from human stakes; #18 puts the village's relationship to a difficult cultural inheritance at the centre. Also, #19's parish-ledger form is too close to comic-sf-1's stenographic-record form — repeating an earlier move would reduce variety in the run set.

It beats #29 because: #29 is the same conflict as #18 in single-scene form. #18 gives the story room to unfold across the festival's preparation and the AIs' distinct approaches; #29 collapses everything to the head-table chair. At 1000–3500 words there's room for #18's wider canvas, and the wider canvas serves the genre's requirement for variation in pacing and texture.

The "centenary-festival comedy is twee" worry is real but addressable in Phase 5 (structure) and Phase 7 (variants):

- The structure should not let the festival itself be the centre — better to use the festival as a horizon, with the AIs' work happening in the preparation, the books, the side-rooms.
- The writer must remain off-stage. The "color-only" rule from `wikipedia-seed.md` is binding: the village honours him; the AIs audit the honour; the writer is the absent centre but never the speaker.
- The MP material (the village, the church, the destroyed-and-rebuilt monument) gives the festival a setting with weight beyond just the writer-figure. The story is about a village commemorating not just a writer but a complicated public memory; the writer is the latest of several inheritances the village handles imperfectly.

Inflections to keep available for Phase 4:

- **GG** (Gravity Group): perhaps a small wooden coaster as part of the heritage zone, mentioned once. Or perhaps a contractor for the festival's stage-build. Low-priority inflection.
- **EC** (Ecliptophanes): perhaps an entomological exhibit at the festival, or a beetle in the church icon screen, mentioned in passing. Low-priority.

**Winner conflict, stated cleanly:**

In Mokro Polje (small village in Šibenik-Knin County, Croatia, pop. 163, with a 16th-century Orthodox Church of St. Luke and the rebuilt-after-Operation-Storm 1952 Partisan monument), the centenary of a Moldovan writer in the Esinencu tradition is to be marked by a small festival. The writer (deceased; off-stage throughout) was famous in life for refusing to attend any celebration in his honour, including his own seventieth-birthday party. The festival's kind organiser-AI must decide whether to honour his refusal posthumously by holding no festival, or to honour it by holding the festival anyway with all his refusals catalogued. A second kind AI — a forensic-accounting AI assigned to the centenary fund — is consulted on whether a posthumous refusal is auditable, and on whether the chair he would have refused to occupy is an asset, a liability, or a pre-existing absence. The story's humans are the village priest, the monument-restoration committee chair, the festival's local-language reader, and the writer's nephew (who sent the festival a sealed envelope marked "open if necessary").
