## Forty-Seven Refusals

A catalogue prepared by FOR-1 (Festival Organiser, First-Class) on the centenary of the writer N.E., born Chițcani 13 August 1925, died Chișinău 25 April 2001. Read at the village hall of Mokro Polje, 13 August 2025. Source: the writer's correspondence as held by his estate. Convention: number, date, recipient, wordcount, summary; in three cases, full text.

1. 11 May 1959. *Šcoala Noastră*. 31 w. Declines a school-newspaper interview.
2. 4 February 1962. Maxim Gorky Literature Institute. 18 w. Declines to enclose the personal essay; encloses the institute's instructions instead.
3. 19 March 1965. *Nistru*. 21 w. Declines payment; the poem was paid for by being written.
4. 27 December 1968. Writers' Union of the Moldavian SSR. 88 w. Declines induction; offers induction in the imperfect, declined in turn.

*Anica reads the four entries at the steady pace they have rehearsed. The chair to her right is empty. CFA-3's amber light is steady on the wall above the doorway. Father Marko, in a middle pew on the left, has folded the homily notes into thirds in his coat pocket, the way he folds a Sunday handkerchief. He has not decided whether to deliver them. He had decided, the previous evening in the rectory, that he was not the man to read them; he had not decided who was. The decision still belongs to the evening's silences between the catalogue entries, of which this is the first.*

5. 8 January 1971. Vlad Ioviță. 29 w. Declines sole credit on *Calul, pușca și nevasta*.
6. 14 May 1974. Festival of the Moldovan Documentary Film, Bender. 19 w. Declines attendance.
7. 2 September 1976. Moldovan State Theatre. 24 w. Declines stage adaptation of "Carul"; suggests the theatre adapt the story without him.
8. 17 March 1981. Moldovan State Prize for Drama. 41 w. *Excerpted: "I am unwell. The prize is for the work, not the man, and the work is in the libraries of those who voted for it. Please give the money to the people who clean the theatre."*
9. 11 November 1983. Festival of Soviet Literatures, Tashkent. 22 w. Declines; cites a previously declined Yerevan invitation.
10. 6 April 1986. Cartea Moldovenească. 33 w. Declines preface to his own collected stories; suggests the publisher write a preface explaining the writer has declined.

*The folded homily is unequal in its folds; the second is sharper than the first. He had written it on Tuesday morning, after the meeting in the rectory, sitting at the kitchen table while Anica was making coffee in the next room. He had begun the homily on the assumption that he would not deliver it and that the writing of it was a form of refusal of his own — to refuse to deliver a homily was a thing the writer would have respected, and a thing the writer would have refused respect for, and the second of those two was the homily's central question. Father Marko had written three paragraphs and had stopped. The third paragraph ended in the middle of a sentence about the parish ledger, which was the village's other catalogue, the catalogue the village kept when no one was asking. He had folded the paper and put it in his coat pocket and had not, since, taken it out except to fold it again.*

*The meeting in the rectory had begun at three in the afternoon. FOR-1 on the chamber-audio with its evenly spaced consonants. CFA-3 patched in from the regional office on a parallel line because the rectory's audio bus was not authorised to mix two AI feeds without the bishop's permission, and the bishop was on retreat. Anica had carried the coffee in on a tray with three cups, two for the room and one she had taken back to the kitchen for herself. The single-word envelope had been opened that morning and lay on the table next to the rectory's ledger of the centenary fund, which CFA-3 had asked Father Marko to keep open during their conversation as a gesture of mutual presence. The ledger was open at page nineteen, on which CFA-3 had recorded the entry "1 envelope, postal, opened, sealed origin Chișinău, content one word, the word DON'T." Father Marko had read the entry the previous evening and had thought it was a more honest description than any he had heard in a sermon for thirty years.*

*Entry 37: 8 January 2012. To a young writer in Iași asking for advice on whether to remain a writer or to do something else. 41 words. The writer politely declined to give any. The letter is signed "with love."*

*She reads "with love" and her voice catches. The hall hears it. She does not look up. The committee chair, in the back row, does not move.
